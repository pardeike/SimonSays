# Simon Says

Simon Says gives your colony strange rules to obey.

Every so often Simon interrupts with a new task: play faster, lose control of the camera, hide colonists, mix up commands, rename everyone Simon, and other rude ideas.

It is a chaos challenge for players who want RimWorld to mess with their habits instead of only attacking their base.
